Evidence carries one of four labels: **[Public]** with source and date, **illustrative model**, **scenario** or **unresolved**. Gaps stay gaps; benchmarks set ranges, not Onelife facts.

## 10 | Join funnel, promotions and digital

**A $1 join offer bought volume in the new states and sent the bill to the 90-day cancel line.** In the demo scenario, the $1 joining-fee offer was extended in North Carolina and Alabama in week 37 with no end date. Fee-waived joins now make up 46% of all joins, and 70% of the excess early cancellations come from $1-fee members at the five NC and AL clubs.

| Funnel stage | Illustrative W41 / TTM | Reading |
|---|---:|---|
| Free-pass and trial registrations | ~9,800 / week | Onelife's real offer is a free pass or free Premier trial |
| Joins | 3,920 / week; 188.7k TTM | 96% of plan (M02 watch) |
| Joining fee waived (M10) | 46% of joins | **Breach**, threshold 30% |

|---|---:|---:|
| Full or $5 join fee, all states | 54% | 8.9% |

**Note on the scenario:** the $1 join offer is a demo construct. On the pages we checked (15 Oct 2026), Onelife offers a free pass or free Premier trial and a $5 Premier joining fee; no $1 offer was visible.

## 11 | Cancellation rules and retention risk

**The federal click-to-cancel rule is gone, but the enforcement risk is not.** The FTC's rule was vacated by the Eighth Circuit in July 2025, days before compliance (Latham & Watkins summary). On 20 Aug 2025 the FTC sued LA Fitness over its cancellation practices (Bloomberg). States keep legislating, and the DC Attorney General previously sued Washington Sports Club over misleading practices, a precedent in Onelife's home market.

| Rule or event [Public] | Date | What it changes |
|---|---|---|
| FTC "click-to-cancel" rule vacated (8th Cir.) | Jul 2025 | No federal online-cancel mandate today |
| FTC v. LA Fitness (cancellation practices) | 20 Aug 2025 | Enforcement under existing law continues |
| State auto-renewal bills (HFA lobbying in 11 states) | 2025–26 | State-by-state cancel duties |
| DC AG v. Washington Sports Club | Earlier action | Local precedent in the DC core |
| Planet Fitness churn reported at 5–6% after earlier click-to-cancel changes, back to ~4.2–4.3% [Unverified, expert-call summary] | 2024–25 | Order-of-magnitude for a rule shock |

**The four EBITDA definitions.** **Reported EBITDA:** earnings before interest, tax, depreciation and amortisation from the general ledger, after all club, central and pre-opening costs ($71.4m). **Covenant EBITDA:** the credit agreement's Consolidated EBITDA; reported EBITDA plus permitted add-backs, here pre-opening costs for clubs not yet open or open 12 months or less, capped at 5% ($75.0m as sent, $73.8m restated). **Run-rate EBITDA:** the owner's view, adding the annualised effect of new clubs, acquisitions and price actions not yet in the trailing period ($79.8m; $78.6m restated); not accepted by a lender. **Lease-adjusted EBITDAR:** covenant EBITDA plus rent, used with debt plus 8x rent for rating and insurance files ($111.8m; $110.6m restated).

**Other terms.** **Four-wall EBITDA:** club revenue less club labour, rent, utilities, marketing and local costs, before central overhead and pre-opening. **Effective dues:** dues revenue collected per average member per month after rate-locks, promotions and freezes. **Attrition:** cancellations in the month ÷ average members. **90-day cancels:** joins cancelled within 90 days ÷ joins. **Net leverage:** (debt − cash) ÷ covenant EBITDA. **FCCR:** (covenant EBITDA − maintenance capex − cash tax) ÷ (cash interest + scheduled amortisation). **Cushion:** the share by which covenant EBITDA can fall before net leverage reaches the maximum. **Lease-adjusted leverage:** (net debt + 8x rent) ÷ EBITDAR.
